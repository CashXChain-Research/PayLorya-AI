What it does

One assistant. Three jobs.

Reads any invoice.

A PDF, an email or plain text. Claude extracts the fields, and nothing else.

  • PDF
  • Email
  • Text

Pays per request.

Each payment-readiness check is bought with x402 on Cardano Preprod, within a price cap and a 20 tADA session budget.

  • 2 tADA per check
  • Cap 3 tADA

Pays suppliers after your OK.

One approval, then the payout goes out to the verified payee. Or set limits and let it pay on its own.

  • tADA on Cardano Preprod
  • CashXChain

CashXChainThe Infrastructure for the money of tomorrow.

Built at the TUM Blockchain & AI Hackathon 2026, Cardano track.

Demo on Cardano Preprod with test funds. Crypto invoices are paid in tADA on Cardano Preprod after your approval. Fiat invoices go to the CashXChain sandbox (a test ledger, no real money), or to a labeled simulation when no sandbox key is set. Corridors, fees and FX are simulated and labeled as such. The report is a data-quality and routing check, not an AML, sanctions or KYB decision.
