1. Controller

The controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is the provider named in the legal notice: CashXChain Inc., 48 Wall Street, Suite 1100, PMB 1024, New York, NY 10005, United States, email legal@cashxchain.com.

2. What PayLorya AI does with data

PayLorya AI is an AI CFO for businesses. You can upload financial documents, chat with the AI CFO, connect wallets and accounts, plan budgets and draft and approve payments. To do this, we process the data described below. We do not sell personal data, do not show ads and do not use tracking or analytics tools.

4. AI processing

To read documents and answer chat messages, the content you submit is sent to the Claude API of Anthropic PBC (USA). Anthropic acts as our processor. According to Anthropic's commercial terms, API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models. AI results can be wrong; the AI CFO does not make decisions that produce legal effects for you on its own. Whether the agent may execute a payment without your approval is decided by rules and limits you set (autonomy level, limits, known payees), not by the model.

5. Recipients and processors

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (USA): hosting, content delivery, database (Cloudflare D1, primary location Western Europe) and file storage for uploaded documents (Cloudflare R2). Server logs are processed by Cloudflare.
  • Anthropic PBC (USA): AI processing of documents and chat messages (section 4).
  • Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC: only if you choose "Continue with Google". Google confirms your identity and sends us your name, email address and profile picture URL; we do not get access to your Google account otherwise.
  • Blockfrost: Cardano blockchain data provider; receives the Cardano addresses whose balances we look up.
  • publicnode.com: public RPC endpoints for Ethereum, Base and Polygon; receive the EVM addresses whose balances we look up.
  • CashXChain UG (haftungsbeschränkt) (Germany), operator of the CashXChain platform: payment rails for fiat payouts (currently a sandbox with test money); receives payee and payment details for payouts you approve or allow.
  • Public blockchains: Cardano transactions (currently on the Preprod test network) are public and permanent by design. They cannot be deleted.

6. Transfers outside the EU

Some processors are located in the USA or may process data there. Transfers are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified, and otherwise on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). Uploaded files are currently stored in a Cloudflare R2 bucket that is not restricted to the EU.

7. Cookies and local storage

We only use what is strictly necessary to run the service:

  • Session cookies (better-auth.session_token and a short-lived signed session cache cookie): keep you signed in. Deleted when you sign out or after the session expires (up to 14 days).
  • Local storage (cxc-theme): remembers whether you chose light or dark mode. Stays in your browser until you clear it.

We do not use cookies for tracking, analytics or advertising. Legal basis: § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG and Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

8. Retention

We keep account and workspace data for as long as your account exists. Sessions expire after 14 days of validity. When you delete documents, payees, budgets or chats, they are removed from our database and file storage. When your account is closed, we delete its data, except where we must keep it to meet legal retention obligations. To close your account, write to legal@cashxchain.com.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR). To exercise them, contact us at legal@cashxchain.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live or work.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as hashes. Uploaded files are served back only to signed-in members of the workspace they belong to. No system is perfectly secure; tell us about suspected vulnerabilities at legal@cashxchain.com.

11. Changes

We will update this policy when the service or the law changes. The date at the top shows the current version.
