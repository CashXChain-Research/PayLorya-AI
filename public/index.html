FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Talk to us.

Can the AI move money on its own?

Only if you allow it. In Read-only it never does. In Guarded it pays known payees within your per-payment limit and monthly budget; everything else waits for your approval. Full access must be confirmed explicitly.

What stops a malicious document from redirecting a payment?

The model only extracts fields and proposes. Payees, limits and budgets come from your settings and are checked by deterministic code, so instructions hidden in a document have nothing to act on.

Which documents can it read?

Invoices, receipts, bank statements and contracts as PDF, photo or CSV. It extracts the fields, categorises the entry and links it to the right payee.

Which accounts can I connect?

Cardano wallets, other chain wallets as watch-only, and your CashXChain account. Bank accounts are coming soon.

What is x402?

An open protocol that puts the price into the HTTP response. When the AI CFO needs a third-party verification, it pays for that one request on Cardano, within a capped price, with no subscription or checkout.

Is this real money?

Not yet. Cardano payments run on the Preprod testnet and CashXChain fiat payments run in the sandbox while we are in early access.

Early access is free. Talk to us about your volumes and use case.
