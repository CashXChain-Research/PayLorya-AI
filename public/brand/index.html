Use

  • Keep clear space around the logo of at least the size of the coral dot, twice over.
  • Smallest size: mark 16 px, horizontal logo 24 px high. Typeface: Manrope (Google Fonts), 400 to 800.
  • Do not recolor, stretch, rotate or add effects. On photos, use the on-dark version on a dark area.
  • Payments run on CashXChain rails; name CashXChain as the rail, not as the product.
